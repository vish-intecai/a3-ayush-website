Our Legacy

A heritage of healing, passed down through four generations

A3 Ayush began as a family of traditional healers more than a century ago. Today we bring that knowledge of Ayurveda, Siddha-rooted Varma therapy and herbal medicine into modern, hygienic clinics.

We look at the whole person: body, lifestyle, diet and mind. That's how we find and treat the root cause of back pain, sciatica, joint problems, respiratory allergies and chronic skin conditions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment before visiting?

We recommend booking so the doctor is available. Hosur has doctors on Tue/Thu/Sat and Bengaluru on Mon/Wed/Fri. Use the form above or WhatsApp us.

Can disc bulge or sciatica be treated without surgery?

Many of our patients with disc bulge, sciatica and spondylosis get significant relief from our non-surgical spinal therapy, which combines Varma, herbal oils and corrective care. The doctor will assess your case at consultation.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the condition and how long you've had it. After the consultation, the doctor will give you a clear plan with the expected duration.

Are Ayurvedic medicines safe?

Our formulations are prepared from traditional herbal recipes and prescribed only after consultation, at the right dose for you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any previous reports, scans (MRI/X-ray) and a list of the medicines you currently take.
